# DiraPro — Product Overview

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Public Draft

---

## 1. Product Definition

DiraPro is a **Professional Identity & Knowledge Platform**.

It helps professionals create a credible digital presence that brings together their identity, expertise, experience, achievements, knowledge, stories, and relevant media.

The initial product is centered around the **DiraPro Professional Profile**.

---

## 2. Core Product

A DiraPro Professional Profile may include:

- Professional identity and introduction
- Career and professional journey
- Expertise and skills
- Education and certifications
- Achievements and recognition
- Articles and professional insights
- Podcasts, interviews, and videos
- Professional services
- Relevant verification

The profile should be more structured and richer than a traditional online biography.

---

## 3. Core User Experience

The initial experience should allow a visitor to:

```text
Discover
   ↓
Understand the Professional
   ↓
Explore Expertise
   ↓
Read / Watch / Listen
   ↓
Build Trust
   ↓
Connect

A professional should be able to create a profile that they are comfortable sharing as their primary professional digital identity.

5. AI Capabilities

AI will be introduced where it creates clear user value.

Potential uses include:

Biography and profile assistance
Content drafting
Content summarization
Knowledge organization
Translation
Search and discovery
Profile recommendations

AI-generated content should remain subject to appropriate human review.

AI amplifies expertise; it does not manufacture credibility.

6. Trust & Verification

Trust is a core product principle.

Where appropriate, DiraPro may support verification of:

Professional identity
Credentials
Certifications
Organizations
Publications
Other professional claims

Verification methods and standards will evolve as the product develops.

DiraPro should clearly distinguish between self-provided information, platform-verified information, and third-party references.

7. Initial MVP

The first public product should focus on:

High-quality professional profiles
Professional discovery
Content and knowledge
Media presentation
Basic administration
Professional onboarding
Responsive web experience

Advanced features should be introduced only after real-world validation.

8. Product Principles
Quality Before Scale

Create excellent professional experiences before expanding the platform.

Simple Before Complex

Prefer simple workflows over unnecessary features.

Human Before AI

Professionals and their expertise remain at the center of the product.

Trust by Design

Accuracy, attribution, consent, and transparency are part of the product.

Build for Evolution

The initial architecture should support future discovery, knowledge, AI, and ecosystem capabilities without over-engineering the MVP.

9. Future Direction

DiraPro may evolve toward:

Advanced professional discovery
AI-powered search
Structured professional knowledge
Verified expertise
Personalized professional experiences
Professional communities
Enterprise knowledge ecosystems

These capabilities remain subject to product validation.

10. Product Success

The initial product is successful if professionals:

- Create a profile
- Are proud to share it
- Find it useful
- Return to maintain it
- Recommend DiraPro to other professionals
- Eventually pay for valuable capabilities
