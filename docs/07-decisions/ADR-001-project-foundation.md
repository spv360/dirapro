# ADR-001 — DiraPro Project Foundation

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Context

DiraPro is being developed as an AI-enabled Professional Identity & Knowledge Platform.

The project will initially focus on a public, high-quality web experience and validation with a small professional community before investing in advanced platform capabilities.

The GitHub repository is public and therefore should contain only information appropriate for public visibility.

## Decision

DiraPro will follow these principles:

1. **Public repository** — Product, brand, development, and public technical documentation.
2. **Private strategy workspace** — Detailed business strategy, pricing, partnerships, financials, customer research, and confidential planning.
3. **Documentation first** — Product and architecture decisions should be documented before significant implementation.
4. **Validation before scale** — Validate professional demand before building complex platform capabilities.
5. **Simple MVP** — Start with professional profiles, discovery, content, media, and basic administration.
6. **AI as an enabler** — Use AI where it provides measurable value without replacing professional authenticity.
7. **Trust by design** — Accuracy, attribution, consent, privacy, and transparency are core product principles.
8. **Global by design** — The initial market may be local, but the product should support future global expansion.

## Public Repository Scope

The public repository may contain:

- Product vision
- Brand guidelines
- Product documentation
- Public roadmap
- Technical architecture
- Development documentation
- Release history

The public repository should not contain:

- Private partnership agreements
- Founder/equity information
- Detailed financial models
- Pricing strategy
- Private customer information
- Confidential competitive intelligence
- Private operational or sales strategies
- Secrets or credentials

## Consequences

This approach allows DiraPro to build publicly and establish technical/product credibility while protecting sensitive business information.

The public repository should communicate **what DiraPro is building**, while the private strategy workspace documents **how the business intends to build and grow it**.
